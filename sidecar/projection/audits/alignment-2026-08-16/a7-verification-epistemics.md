## 2 The domain space (independent of current code)

The plane's job is to mint, carry, and retire **knowledge claims** about an estate. Every claim in the domain has:
- **Subject** (what it is about: a kind/column/relationship/table/flow/estate) and **grain** (per-env, cross-env, estate-wide, artifact-wide).
- **Epistemic pedigree**: how known (observed rows / catalog reflection / journal claims / declared model / assumed default), observed when, through what basis (live wire, verified cache, unprobed store), at what strength (above/below decision floor; symmetric/asymmetric samples), and **what would falsify it** (a reopen probe, a fingerprint move, a re-run).
- **Disposition** (what it is FOR): needs a ruling / has a mechanical repair / is an interim posture / is advisory — and the disposition may be **instance-graded** (the same hazard is watchable at 40% and ruling-grade at 90% headroom; the contract's dual-lane rows say so).
- **Detection status over the whole declared vocabulary**: detected today / named-not-yet-detected / deliberately-not-detected (carried faithfully, no check needed) / suppressed-by-posture (detected, owned by another line) / undetectable-in-principle (XML has no checksum). Honest coverage = the complement of the detected set, derived.
- **Verdict/exit geometry**: verdicts at several grains (estate, environment, proof, canary, gate streak) that must compose without a second vocabulary; refusals named (exit 6), forks named (exit 5); silence (a zero) meaningful only when observation itself is on the record — observed-zero ≠ unmeasured is a domain distinction wherever a zero is a guarantee.
- **Freshness** is one decision algebra everywhere evidence is reused: recorded reading × live probe × policy × operator override → reuse | re-read, with the miss reason a named value.
- **Law-witness bookkeeping**: every law's verification state (verified-here / verified-elsewhere-by-name / structural-only / deferred-with-trigger / unbacked) machine-walkable, since the system's own creed is "every correctness claim a property test."

### A7-1 — The finding is a knowledge claim; its pedigree is prose (M6, EPISTEMIC)

**Evidence.** `Finding` (Estate.fs:86–107) carries `Envs : (string * int64) list` — bare env-name strings paired with a weight whose *semantics vary by kind* (NULL rows, orphan rows, channel-change count, `1L` presence markers, rivals-count at 2210, `[]` = estate-wide for proof findings, 2129–2131 — so `weightOf` (110–111) ranks heterogeneous quantities, and proof findings rank at weight 0). Everything else the domain knows about the claim is composed into `Statement` at mint time (1984–1993): the satisfied-environment clause with its observation basis (`cleanClause`, 1856–1891), the below-decision-floor demotion, the majority note, the difficulty clause, the fork note. The fork itself survives only as `Fork : bool`; the witness (which signatures diverge) is a `%A` render compared then dropped (416–425, 506, 1943–1951). Firm-vs-advisory standing is derived **at render** by partitioning `EnvBasis` (2381–2398) and stated as a sentence — "verdicts leaning on the advisory environment(s) are advisory too" — while `environments.json` emits findings with no advisory/standing field (2700–2718); a CI consumer keying on `lanes.decide > 0` cannot tell a firm finding from one standing wholly on 9-day-old unprobed evidence, and cannot recover the floor/asymmetry demotions except by parsing English. The masthead's Cached copy also over-claims uniformly ("content-verified fresh… across N kind(s)", 2366) though an XML-only kind carries no content hash (CLAUDE.md §4.15; KindFingerprint.ContentHash `option`, EstateEvidenceStore.fs:37–43) — the per-kind basis exists in the type and is erased in the roll-up.

**Misalignment.** The domain's epistemic axes (basis, age, strength, falsifier) are reified once at the *environment* grain (`EnvBasis`/`EvidenceProvenance` — good) but never joined to the claim grain; the claim's standing is reconstructable only from prose. `Relaxation.Evidence : (string * int64) list` (EstateFinding.fs:899–903) repeats the untyped shape.

**Candidate primitive.** `EvidenceStanding` on `Finding` (and in the JSON): `{ Env : EnvName; Magnitude : FindingMagnitude (Rows|Changes|Presence|Rivals); Standing : Firm | BelowFloor of observed:int64 | AsymmetricSmallSide | OfflineBasis | NoEvidence }` — one value per contributing environment; `Statement` becomes a projection of it. (An `EnvName` VO would end the bare-string env identity across `Envs`/`Posture`/`StaticContent.ByEnv` in the same stroke — the one identity in this plane without a type, against the house "identity as a type" gravity.)

**Fluency bought.** Machine-filterable advisory findings; honest impact ranking within one magnitude sort; the board's honesty sentences derived, never composed ad hoc; the JSON stops being a prose transport. **Effort:** M. **Risk of inaction:** external consumers (CI, the rich board, future SSIS-side tooling) inherit over-claims silently; every new demotion rule becomes another sentence fragment in an already five-part `sprintf`.

## 4 Anti-findings (correct specializations)

- **`DetectionStatus` staying binary** (EstateFinding.fs:81–91): the tempting third case "detected-but-suppressed" is *correctly* absent — posture suppression is finding-grain routing, not kind-grain status: a relaxed violation returns `None` at the detector (Estate.fs:624–625, 642) because `PostureActive` owns the fact ("one fact never renders twice"), which is the suppression *reified as a kind on the RELAX lane*, richer than a status flag. "Deliberately-not-detected" (compression/sequences/PERSISTED carried faithfully) was rightly expressed by *retiring the kinds* rather than a third status — no detector is owed for a non-hazard.
